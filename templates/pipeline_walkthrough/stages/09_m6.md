# 09 Equivalence Validator

What the Equivalence Validator reads:
- plan: `../shared/optimizer_outputs/m5_execution_plan.json`
- typed DAG: `../shared/optimizer_outputs/m4_typed_dag.json`
- profile: `../shared/profile_builder_output/ha_profile.json`
- compare results / replay outputs: `../shared/optimizer_outputs/m6_trace_compare.jsonl`

What the Equivalence Validator produces:
- `../shared/optimizer_outputs/counterexamples.json`
- `../shared/optimizer_outputs/rule_hardening_decisions.json`
- `../shared/optimizer_outputs/execution_certificate.json`

Why it exists:
- validates the optimized behavior, records counterexamples, applies rollback/hardening and emits a certificate
